
# ROE Protocol

Decentralized educational content licensing and automated creator revenue sharing powered by Solana and The Roe Vault.

## The Problem

Educational creators rely on centralized platforms for payments, distribution and revenue sharing.

## Our Solution

ROE Protocol enables experts to register educational products, receive automated payments and establish verifiable purchase records on Solana.

## Revenue Model

- 70% goes directly to the expert.
- 30% goes directly to The Roe Vault.
- Payments are distributed automatically.

## Working Features

- On-chain educational product registration
- Automated SOL payment distribution
- On-chain purchase receipts
- Sales tracking
- Duplicate purchase prevention mechanism

## Technology

- Solana
- Rust
- Anchor Framework
- TypeScript
